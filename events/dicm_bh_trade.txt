# Scope rules:
# - ROOT is the acting country throughout construction.
# - every_owned_province and the best-province target enter province scope.
# - owner returns to country scope; "which = ROOT" copies a same-named country variable.
#
# Building branches supply literal IDs and constants to shared processing helpers.
